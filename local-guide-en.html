Steps

  1. Set up (once)

    Put your name and email in the "Customer sheet" tab, so the sheet shows your contact info.

  2. Pick 5 shops

    On Google Maps, look for shops that match all four, and send the names to your teammate.

    • No "Website" button on their Google Maps listing
    • 20 or more reviews (they have regular customers)
    • An Instagram or Facebook page (so we have photos)
    • Not a chain

  3. We make previews

    The AI makes a preview for each shop that you can show on your phone (1 to 2 days).

  4. Visit the shops

    • For restaurants and cafés, go on a weekday after school, before 5 p.m. Avoid the lunch and dinner rush, and weekends
    • Don't go into shops with a "No Soliciting" sign
    • If the owner isn't there, ask the staff when they're usually in, and come back then
    • Show the preview and the customer sheet on your phone. See "What to say"
    • Even if they don't decide, leave a printed customer sheet

  5. When they say yes

    The AI writes a short agreement (what we'll do and the price). You collect half the payment, then get the menu, photos, hours, and logo (if they have one). For the menu, a phone photo of the paper menu or board is enough.

  6. We build it

    Your teammate sends everything to the AI. The AI builds the site and sends a link. Show it to the owner and collect changes (two rounds).

  7. Go live

    The shop registers its domain in its own name (we send step-by-step instructions). We put the site online. The owner adds the web address to their Google Maps listing on their own phone. Give them the QR sign and collect the second half.

  8. After launch

    Offer the update plan. If they're happy, ask for a Google review, and whether they know another shop that could use a website.

Things we don't do

  • Ask for the owner's passwords. They add the site to Google Maps themselves, on their own phone
  • Show fake reviews, or work we didn't do
  • Keep pushing. If they say no, thank them and leave

What is a review card?

  • A card that opens the shop's Google review page when a customer taps their phone on it. No searching, so it's easier to ask for reviews
  • Price: 2 cards for $39 (one for the counter, one for the tables), extra cards $15 each, two free with a website. No monthly fee
  • Payment: all of it when they say yes. By e-Transfer, to you
  • Buying cards: search AliExpress for "NFC Google review card NTAG215 blank", 10 cards (about $2 each, 2 to 4 weeks to arrive). The chip should be NTAG213 or 215, and blank
  • For shops with a website, the card can open the website instead (we add Google review, Instagram and Facebook buttons and news to the site)

The day before you visit

  1. Pick a shop

    On the shop map, look for shops with few Google reviews (under about 50) that still seem to get customers.

  2. Make the shop's review link

    Open Google's "Place ID Finder" (a map page on developers.google.com), type the shop's name and copy the Place ID. Paste it after https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=

  3. Write it to a card

    Free app "NFC Tools" → Write → Add a record → URL → paste the link → Write → hold the card to the top edge of the iPhone.

  4. Check it

    Tap your own iPhone on it. If the shop's review page opens, you're done. Don't lock it yet (if they don't buy, you can rewrite it for another shop).

  • Ask for the shop's Google password

The customer sheet

One page that explains what we do, what's included, how it works, and the prices. Show it on your phone, or print it and leave it at the shop.

Add your name and contact info first. They're only saved on this device. You can leave the phone number blank.
