③ Description & FAQ

Description（1,200字以内）
Want Japanese shoppers to actually buy from your Shopify store?

Translating your page word for word is not enough. Japanese buyers expect a calm tone, tax-included prices, clear delivery days and simple return info. Stiff machine translation sounds pushy, and people leave.

I'm a native Japanese speaker. I have run my own online store, and I'm now studying how culture changes the way people shop. I rewrite your store so it reads like it was made in Japan.

What I localize:
- Product titles and descriptions
- Homepage and collection headlines, banners and buttons
- Prices, shipping and return wording
- Store policies and FAQ
- Japanese keywords shoppers really search

How it works:
1. Send your store link or text, and tell me about your customers
2. I localize everything into natural Japanese
3. You get a ready-to-paste sheet that matches your Shopify fields

Every line is checked by a native speaker.

If your store is large, message me before ordering and I'll send you a custom offer.
FAQ 1
Do you use machine translation?
I may use tools for a first draft, but every line is rewritten and checked by me, a native Japanese speaker. You get natural Japanese, not raw machine translation.
FAQ 2
Will you upload the text to my store?
I deliver a sheet that matches your Shopify fields (title, description, SEO title and so on), so you can paste everything in a few minutes.
FAQ 3
Can you translate the text on my banners and images?
Yes. Send me the image or the text on it, and I will give you the Japanese version. You or your designer can then update the image.
